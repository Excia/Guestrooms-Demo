## Placeholders and invented values

- **P-151-1:** a room's party limit is `MAX_PARTY` (20) until the room's maximum occupancy exists (`src/api/validation.ts`, Q-151-2).
- **P-151-2:** the mock splits a reservation's old counts over its rooms the first time it needs them: every room gets at least one adult, the rest and all children go to the first room (`ensureParties`, `src/api/reservationDetailData.ts`). Seeded multi-room reservations therefore show e.g. "2 Adults 2 Children" in room 1 and "1 Adult" in the others.
- **P-151-3:** "Confirmation sent" is only a flag in the mock; no email is sent.

## API, permissions, notes

- **API:** `POST /reservations/{id}/rooms`, `PUT /reservation-rooms/{id}/guests`, `PUT /reservations/{id}/notes`; section "Edit panel" in `api-contract.md`. New optional fields on `ReservationRoomDetail`.
- **Permissions:** `reservation.edit` for guests and notes. None for Add Another Room (D-151-4).
- **Notes:** the "Edit Room" and "Edit Reservation" notices and the other stubs of the details panel are unchanged. The Add Another Room menu item no longer shows a notice; it opens the panel. Not verified in a real browser window in this session (the browser tool timed out); the behavior is covered by `Schedule.editPanel.test.tsx`.
