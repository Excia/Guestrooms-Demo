## API, permissions, notes

- **API:** `schedule.holdSlot` (`PUT /schedule/holds/{holdId}`), `schedule.releaseHold` (`DELETE /schedule/holds/{holdId}`), `ScheduleData.holds`, `CreateReservationRequest.holdId`; see "Slot holds (ticket 152)" in `api-contract.md`. A real backend must own holds and their expiry.
- **Permissions:** placing a hold needs `reservation.edit` (the mock answers 403 without it); reading holds needs none beyond the schedule page.
- **Notes:** rules are pure and tested in `src/app/slotHolds.ts` (expiry, overlap with booked, closed and held slots, ownership); the mock in `src/api/holdData.ts`; the panel hook `useSlotHold`; the grid block `ScheduleHoldView`; `useLiveHolds` redraws at the next expiry. The `Schedule` page takes an injectable `now` (epoch ms) for tests.
