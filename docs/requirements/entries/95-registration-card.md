## Placeholders and invented values

- **P-95-1:** the default custom text in `src/api/systemSettingsData.ts` (`mockSystemSettings`), the sample reservation and "Pine Ridge Club" in `src/api/registrationCardData.ts`. The mock renders a printable HTML page instead of a PDF, with colors, fonts and sizes written into its inline stylesheet (a stand-in outside the MUI theme; the backend's PDF follows Figma 15957:187196); the backend renders the real PDF.

## API, permissions, notes

- **API:** `GET` and `PUT /settings/system` carry `registrationCard`; new `POST /registration-card/preview` (see the section "Registration Card (ticket 7.8)" in `api-contract.md`). Print Registration Card(s) on a reservation sends the saved settings and the reservation (`reservation` in the same preview request, mocked) and opens the result; the reservation detail has no lead occupant phone or address or separate reserver yet, so those print empty until the backend builds the card itself.
- **Permissions:** none new (admin routes only, ticket 0.8).
- **Notes:** the rules are in `src/app/registrationCard.ts` so the backend's generator can mirror them (`buildRegistrationCard` is the reference).
