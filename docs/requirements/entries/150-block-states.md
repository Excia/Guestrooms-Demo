## Placeholders and invented values

- **P-150-1:** `palette.schedule.pastDate`, `dropColumn` and the `opacity` values (`src/theme.ts`) are not from Figma variables; the designer confirms them (Q-150-1, Q-150-2).

## API, permissions, notes

- **API:** none. **Permissions:** none.
- **Notes:** "Today" comes from `useClubToday` / `useClubDay` (`src/app/useClubToday.ts`): the club's date from System Settings, the browser's until they load, re-read every minute and on window focus or tab visibility so a page left open changes day at the club's midnight. The Schedule page (default range and today column), New Reservation search, Quick Stats, DateField and DateSelection use it; an explicit `today` prop still wins. Quick Stats fixes its default periods at mount. With no `?start=` and no `today` prop, the Schedule holds its first fetch until the settings have loaded or failed (skeleton meanwhile), so the range never jumps; a `?start=` is never moved. A server-sent `today` (409 `stale`) still wins while a panel is open. The text contrast of the past-date header text is covered by `scheduleColors.contrast.test.ts`.
