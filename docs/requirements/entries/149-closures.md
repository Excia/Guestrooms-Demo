## API, permissions, notes

- **API:** `POST /schedule/closures`, `PUT /schedule/closures/{id}`, `DELETE /schedule/closures/{id}` (see the "Room closures" section of `api-contract.md`); mock and http clients behind `ScheduleApi.createClosure / updateClosure / deleteClosure`.
- **Permissions:** `reservation.edit` (Q-149-5).
- **Notes:** the room of a closure cannot be changed in the details panel (the title names it; the documents list start, end and description only). Duplicate is the way to put the same closure in another room. The reservation panel's room field gained `allowUnassigned={false}` for the closure form.
